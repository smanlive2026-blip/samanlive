What is FSSAI?

FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) is government authority that regulates food safety in India. Any business that sells food must have FSSAI license/registration.

FSSAI license ensures shop follows food safety standards, hygiene, and quality.

Why FSSAI License Important?

✅ Ensures food safety and hygiene

✅ Shop follows government food standards

✅ Customer trust - licensed shops are verified

✅ Legal compliance - required by law for food businesses

✅ Quality assurance - regular inspections

FSSAI License Types

1. Basic Registration: For small food businesses with turnover up to ₹12 lakhs per year. Form A.

2. State License: For medium businesses with turnover ₹12 lakhs to ₹20 crores. Form B - State.

3. Central License: For large businesses with turnover above ₹20 crores or operating in multiple states. Form B - Central.

Most kirana, bakery, restaurant, fruit, dairy shops need at least Basic Registration or State License.

Shops That Need FSSAI

Following shop types must have FSSAI license on SamanLive:

🛒 Kirana, Grocery (if selling food items)

🍎 Fruit, Vegetable, Sabji, Chakki

🥛 Dairy, Bakery, Sweets, Halwai, Juice

🍕 Restaurant, Pizza, Tea, Pansari

🥩 Meat, Fish (if applicable)

🍯 Achar, Masala, Patanjali, etc (food items)

Non-food shops (cloth, mobile, hardware, etc) do not need FSSAI.

How to Get FSSAI License?

Step 1: Go to FSSAI website: foscos.fssai.gov.in

Step 2: Create account and apply for license/registration.

Step 3: Fill Form A (basic) or Form B (state/central) with business details.

Step 4: Upload documents: ID proof, address proof, business proof, etc.

Step 5: Pay fees (₹100 for basic registration, ₹2000-₹7500 for license).

Step 6: FSSAI officer will verify and issue license in 7-30 days.

Documents Needed: Photo, ID proof (Aadhar), address proof, business registration, etc.

Customer - How to Check FSSAI?

On shop page, check for FSSAI badge and license number. You can verify license on FSSAI website: foscos.fssai.gov.in → Verify License.

If shop sells food but no FSSAI license shown, you can report via app: Shop Page → Report Issue → No FSSAI License.

We take food safety seriously. Shops without FSSAI for food business may be suspended.

FSSAI License Renewal

FSSAI license valid for 1-5 years (you choose). Renew before expiry to avoid penalty.

Renewal process same as application - apply on FSSAI website 30 days before expiry.

SamanLive reminds shops 30 days before FSSAI expiry via notification.
