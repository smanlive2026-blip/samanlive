Fast Local DeliveryWe partner with local shops for fast delivery - typically 30-60 mins in Surat.
1. Delivery Areas

1.1 Current Coverage: SamanLive currently delivers in Surat city and nearby areas. Coverage expanding.

1.2 Shop-wise Delivery: Each shop sets its own delivery area and radius (typically 5-10 km).

1.3 Check Availability: Enter your address to see shops delivering to your location.

1.4 Out of Area: If your address is outside shop's delivery area, you may need to pickup from shop or order from another shop.

2. Delivery Time

2.1 Estimated Time: Typically 30-60 mins for most orders in Surat. Depends on:

• Distance between shop and delivery address

• Shop preparation time (food takes longer than kirana)

• Traffic, weather, peak hours

• Number of orders shop has

2.2 Time Estimates:

• Kirana, fruits, vegetables: 30-45 mins

• Food, bakery, restaurant: 45-60 mins

• Clothes, electronics, etc: 60-120 mins (if available for delivery)

2.3 Peak Hours: During festivals, weekends, peak hours (7-10 PM), delivery may take longer.

2.4 Delays: We will notify you if delay expected. You can track order live in app.

3. Delivery Charges

3.1 Free Delivery: Many shops offer free delivery over minimum order (e.g., ₹200, ₹300). Check shop page.

3.2 Delivery Fee: If order below minimum, delivery fee charged (typically ₹20-₹50 based on distance).

3.3 Shop Sets Charges: Delivery charges set by individual shops, not SamanLive.

3.4 Distance-based: Longer distance may have higher delivery charge.

3.5 Surge Charges: During peak demand, rain, festivals, surge charge may apply (will be shown before order).

4. Delivery Process

4.1 Order Confirmation: Shop confirms order and starts preparing.

4.2 Out for Delivery: Delivery boy picks up and goes for delivery. You can track live.

4.3 Delivery Boy Details: You will receive delivery boy name, phone, and live location.

4.4 OTP Verification: For some orders, OTP required at delivery for security. Share OTP only to delivery boy.

4.5 Contact: Delivery boy may call you for address help. Please keep phone available.

5. Customer Responsibilities

5.1 Accurate Address: Provide complete, accurate delivery address with landmark, floor, etc.

5.2 Phone Availability: Keep phone available and answer calls from delivery boy/shop.

5.3 Be Available: Be available at delivery address to receive order.

5.4 Check Order: Please check order at delivery for any damage, missing items, and report immediately.

5.5 COD: For COD, keep exact change ready if possible.

6. Failed Delivery

6.1 Customer Not Available: If you are not available, not answering phone, delivery boy will wait 5-10 mins and try to contact. If still not reachable, order returned to shop.

6.2 Wrong Address: If address is wrong, incomplete, delivery may fail. Customer responsible for providing correct address.

6.3 Re-delivery: One re-delivery attempt may be made. If fails again, order cancelled. Delivery charge non-refundable, product amount refunded (if not perishable).

6.4 Perishable Orders: For fruits, vegetables, food, dairy, if delivery fails, product may be discarded. Refund as per return policy.

7. Self Pickup

7.1 Pickup Option: Some shops offer self pickup - you can order and pickup from shop.

7.2 No Delivery Charge: For self pickup, no delivery charge.

7.3 Pickup Time: Shop will notify when order ready for pickup. Please pickup within 2 hours.

7.4 Bring Order ID: Show order ID or phone number at shop for pickup.

8. Delivery Partners

8.1 Shop Delivery: Most shops deliver via their own staff or delivery boys.

8.2 SamanLive Delivery: In some areas, SamanLive delivery partners deliver.

8.3 Third-party: Shops may use third-party delivery services. SamanLive not responsible for third-party delivery issues, but will help mediate.

9. Tracking

9.1 Live Tracking: Track your order live in app: Profile → My Orders → Track Order.

9.2 Updates: You will receive SMS/WhatsApp/push notifications for order status: confirmed, preparing, out for delivery, delivered.

9.3 Map: See delivery boy live location on map and estimated arrival time.
