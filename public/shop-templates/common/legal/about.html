🎯 Our Mission

To empower local shops and make local shopping easy, fast, and convenient for everyone. We believe in Support Local, Shop Local.

Local shops are heart of our community. They provide fresh products, personal service, and keep our local economy strong. SamanLive brings them online so they can compete with big e-commerce and serve customers better.

💡 What is SamanLive?

SamanLive is a hyperlocal marketplace platform that connects customers with nearby local shops - kirana, fruits, vegetables, bakery, medical, clothes, electronics, and 50+ categories.

Customers can order from local shops online and get fast delivery (30-60 mins) or self pickup. Shops get online presence, more customers, and easy order management.

We are not a big e-commerce company selling our own products. We are a platform that helps YOUR local shops go online.

🚀 Our Story

SamanLive started in 2024 in Surat with a simple idea: Help local shops go online and make local shopping easy.

We saw local shops struggling during COVID and with big e-commerce competition. Customers wanted to support local but no easy way to order online from local shops.

So we built SamanLive - a platform where any local shop can go online in 5 mins, without technical knowledge, without high commission, and serve customers better.

Today, 1000+ shops in Surat are online with SamanLive, serving 10k+ customers, delivering fresh products fast.

Our goal: Make every local shop in India online and make local shopping the first choice for everyone.
