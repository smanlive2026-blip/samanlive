Your Privacy is ImportantWe respect your privacy and protect your data. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.
1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information: When you create account, we collect name, phone, email, address, location.

1.2 Shop Information: Shop owners provide shop name, address, products, prices, FSSAI license, GST, etc.

1.3 Order Information: We collect order details, products ordered, delivery address, payment method.

1.4 Location Data: To show nearby shops and for delivery, we collect precise location with your permission.

1.5 Device Information: We collect device type, OS, app version, IP address, for analytics and security.

1.6 Usage Data: How you use app, pages visited, products viewed, searches, for improving experience.

1.7 Payment Information: Payments processed securely via third-party gateways. We do not store card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 To Provide Services: To create accounts, process orders, delivery, payments, support.

2.2 To Improve Experience: To personalize, recommend products, show nearby shops, improve app.

2.3 Communication: To send order updates, delivery status, offers, support messages via SMS, WhatsApp, email, push.

2.4 Safety & Security: To prevent fraud, fake reviews, abuse, and ensure platform safety.

2.5 Marketing: To send offers, promotions, new features, only if you opt-in. You can opt-out anytime.

2.6 Legal Compliance: To comply with laws, court orders, and enforce terms.

3. Sharing Your Information

3.1 With Shops: When you order, we share name, phone, address, order details with shop for fulfillment.

3.2 With Delivery Partners: We share delivery address, phone, order details with delivery boys.

3.3 With Service Providers: Payment gateways, SMS providers, analytics, cloud hosting, only to provide services.

3.4 Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect rights, we may share information.

3.5 Business Transfer: In merger, acquisition, or sale, information may be transferred with notice.

3.6 We Never Sell Your Data: We never sell your personal data to third parties for marketing.

4. Data Security

4.1 Security Measures: We use encryption, secure servers, access controls to protect your data.

4.2 Payment Security: Payments processed via PCI-DSS compliant gateways. We don't store card details.

4.3 However: No internet transmission is 100% secure. We strive to protect but cannot guarantee absolute security.

4.4 Your Responsibility: Keep account password secure, don't share OTP, logout from shared devices.

5. Your Rights

5.1 Access: You can view and edit your personal information in profile settings.

5.2 Delete: You can delete account via settings. Some data may be retained for legal, fraud prevention.

5.3 Opt-out: You can opt-out of marketing communications via settings or unsubscribe link.

5.4 Location: You can disable location permission anytime, but nearby shops feature may not work.

5.5 Data Portability: You can request copy of your data via email to privacy@samanlive.com

6. Cookies & Tracking

6.1 Cookies: We use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, analyze usage, for ads.

6.2 Types: Essential cookies (login), functional (preferences), analytics (usage), marketing (offers).

6.3 You Can Disable: Via browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

7. Children's Privacy

7.1 Platform is not for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7.2 If you are under 18, please use only with parent/guardian guidance.

7.3 If we learn we collected data from child, we will delete it promptly.

8. Data Retention

8.1 We retain data as long as account is active and as needed to provide services.

8.2 Order data retained for 5 years for legal, tax, accounting purposes.

8.3 After account deletion, some data may be retained for fraud prevention, legal compliance, up to 1 year.

9. International Users

9.1 SamanLive is operated from India. Data stored in India.

9.2 If you use from outside India, you consent to data transfer to and processing in India.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

10.1 We may update this policy from time to time. Changes notified via app/website/email.

10.2 Continued use after changes means acceptance of new policy.

10.3 Please review this page regularly.
